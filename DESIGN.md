# Design System: Julian Dower Portfolio

## Direction
A luminous, atmospheric portfolio inspired by clear skies, sculptural cloud formations and refractive glass. The experience opens inside the atmosphere, then gives the work a calm, legible surface to sit on. Product content and destinations remain the focus.

## Visual language
- Sky blue, ice white and deep ocean blue; use white type over the sky and dark blue over pale surfaces.
- Manrope is the single type family. Large, tightly composed names contrast with small, precise navigation and generous body copy.
- Translucent navigation and controls use soft highlights, subtle blur and rounded silhouettes to suggest glass. Project illustrations remain explicitly labelled as illustrative.
- Procedural clouds respond to pointer and scroll movement; atmospheric effects respect reduced-motion preferences. All content and navigation remain usable if graphics are unavailable.
- Desktop uses a spacious full-height opening and a two-column project gallery. Narrow screens stack the work and preserve readable spacing and touch targets.
